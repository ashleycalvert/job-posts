# Lead/Staff Data Engineer Development Guide

This folder turns the XYZ job-posting analysis into a practical operating guide for an engineer moving from Senior toward Lead or Staff scope. It is designed for someone who is still strengthening parts of the technical and non-technical quality bar while taking on broader responsibility.

## The central idea

The transition is not from “writes code” to “attends more meetings.” It is from **personally completing difficult work** to **making difficult work clearer, safer, and easier for a group to complete and operate**.

At XYZ, the evidence suggests two overlapping paths:

| Path | Primary accountability | Typical scope | Success looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead | Delivery and technical direction for a named platform, program, or team | One important domain or initiative | The team predictably ships and operates a sound system |
| Staff | Architecture and engineering leverage across a complex system or multiple teams | Cross-team or platform-wide | Several teams make better decisions and move faster because of reusable patterns, standards, and guidance |

These are scope patterns, not a clean title ladder. Both remain hands-on; one XYZ Lead posting explicitly describes the role as 70–80% hands-on. Staff is usually a senior individual-contributor role, although a few postings include formal management.

## How to use this material

1. Read [lead_staff_operating_guide.md](lead_staff_operating_guide.md) to understand the mindset, quality bar, and ways of working.
2. Complete the baseline in [growth_plan.md](growth_plan.md) with your manager. Select one real project as the development vehicle; do not create a side project disconnected from team outcomes.
3. Use [working_templates.md](working_templates.md) on that project. The artifacts are mechanisms for clearer thinking, not paperwork to produce for its own sake.
4. Review [evidence_map.md](evidence_map.md) when you need to connect a recommendation to the source analysis or an XYZ requisition.
5. Reassess monthly using demonstrated evidence: decisions made, risks retired, production outcomes, people enabled, and stakeholder confidence.

## Evidence boundary

This guide uses only XYZ Markdown postings whose filenames begin with `R-`, plus the existing analysis in [`analytics/`](../analytics/). It intentionally excludes external postings. The supplied set contains 36 files, of which 35 are substantive; it includes two near-duplicate pairs and one boilerplate-only posting. It is a useful demand sample, not XYZ’s complete career framework.

Because titles, grades, and experience thresholds overlap, the recommendations treat responsibilities and scope as stronger evidence than years or title. They should be calibrated against the expectations of your manager and organization.

## What progress should feel like

Early progress will not feel like knowing everything. It will look like:

- fewer surprises because you expose assumptions and risks early;
- clearer decisions because you compare options against business and operational constraints;
- higher-quality delivery because testing, security, governance, observability, cost, and support are designed in;
- less dependence on you because you create context, patterns, and capable owners;
- greater trust because status is concise, candid, and tied to outcomes;
- better judgment about when to implement, investigate, involve an expert, simplify, or stop.

That is the operating foundation for both Lead and Staff work.
